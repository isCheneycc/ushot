# Ushot 0.1.13

- When history is enabled, saves final edits before closing an editor or pinned image, or quitting Ushot. Keeps content available for retry if saving fails.
- Keeps ongoing edits synchronized when reopening the same history screenshot, preventing newer changes from being overwritten.
- Prevents pending autosaves from recreating deleted or cleared history items.
- Fixes misplaced or ineffective Blur and Mosaic effects after resizing a capture region, and refreshes affected previews when reopening history screenshots.
- Fixes smart-snap selection unexpectedly entering confirmation after cancellation with Esc.
- Requires active capture, editing or output work to finish before a language change or settings reset can restart Ushot.
- Removes the inactive log-level option from Advanced settings.
